📱 What is Likejacking?

Likejacking is a malicious Clickjacking technique where social media "Like", "Share", or "OAuth Authorize" buttons are embedded into transparent iframes aligned over irresistible bait (gameplay trailers, exclusive streams, free cash). The click hijacks the victim's authenticated identity.

🦠 The Viral Worm Effect

🛡️ Modern Defenses

Header Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors 'none' or 'self' reliably prevents all framing. Additionally, setting SameSite=Lax or SameSite=Strict on session cookies ensures credentials are never attached to cross-site iframe requests.
